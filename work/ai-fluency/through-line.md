**Ten options, sharpened down:**

1. I build ML systems that survive honest testing, not just impressive demos.
2. My models keep the number that holds up, even when it's the worse one.
3. I test my results the way they'll actually be used, not the way that flatters them.
4. Real data is messy — I show you exactly how I handle that mess.
5. I don't ship a result until I've tried to break it first.
6. Every claim on this site is one I can rerun in front of you.
7. I build the honest version of the model, not the impressive one.
8. Junior, yes. But I already know when a good score is lying to you.
9. I catch my own model's mistakes before a client ever could.
10. I turn messy real-world data into results that hold up.

**Picked and sharpened:**

> **I don't ship a model until I've tried to break it — and I show you what happened when I did.**

Why this one: it's the only option that's a promise about *behavior*, not a description of skill — "I build ML systems" is a claim anyone could type; "I try to break it before I ship it" is something a visitor can go verify against my actual case studies (the FlyRank client-holdout story is literally me breaking my own result). Memorable because it's slightly aggressive and very specific.
